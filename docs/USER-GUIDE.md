## Admin

Open the staff link while signed in to your school Google account. The **Home** page is a numbered list of the five steps of the project. Each step says how many are waiting and has a button for it. You can run the whole project from this page, top to bottom.

### Step 1. Add students and Chromebooks

1. On Home press **Import a list**.
2. Under *Students and parents* press **Download the blank template** and fill it in, one row per student. You can also copy rows straight from Excel or Google Sheets and paste them.
3. Choose the file (or paste the rows) and press **Check the list**. Nothing is saved yet. Each row is marked:
   - **New**: will be added.
   - **Change**: this student ID is already in the system. The row shows what would change. Tick **Save this change** for the ones you want.
   - **Problem**: a required box is empty, an email is not valid, the grade is outside 5 to 12, or the same ID is in the file twice.
4. Press **Save**. Press **Download the rows left out** to get a file of the problem rows, fix them, and import that file.

Do the same under *Chromebooks* with the Digital Promise file. It needs an Asset Tag column and a Serial Number column.

An import never deletes anyone, never changes a signed agreement, and never sends email.

To add one student, press **Add one student** on Home. To correct a parent's email or phone later, open the student, choose **More actions**, then **Edit student and parent details**. The old and new value are kept in the student's history.

The date of birth column is optional. If you fill it in, a family that lost its link can find the student with the student ID and birth date at the parent link followed by `?page=find`.

### Step 2. Send each family its link

- **Send the link to N families** shows the number of emails and the first email before anything goes out. Press **Send** to confirm.
- Students with no parent email are counted separately. Press **Print QR letters** and send the letter home, or press **Show these students**, open one, and use **Copy the link** to text it or read it out by phone.
- To email only some students, go to **Students**, tick them, and press **Email their link**.

If Gmail reaches its daily limit, the app stops, tells you how many are waiting, and sends them itself the next morning.

### Step 3. Remind families who have not signed

**Send a reminder to N families** emails everyone who has the link and has not signed. **Show who has not signed** opens the list.

### Step 4. Deployment day

- **Open the deployment screen** is the screen everyone uses on the day. See Station Helper below.
- **List of students who are not ready** can be printed or downloaded.
- Before the day, go to **Settings, People**, add the helpers, and press **Switch all helpers on**. Afterwards press **Switch all helpers off**.

### Step 5. After deployment

- **Export for Asset Tiger** downloads the new check-outs, with "Elementary Student" for grade 5 and "Secondary Student" for grades 6 to 12. Import the file into Asset Tiger, then press **They are in Asset Tiger now**.
- **Inventory report** opens the Chromebook list, where you can download the inventory as a spreadsheet or PDF for the twice-yearly VILS count.
- **Reports** shows progress by grade, the claims rate against the 20% VILS limit, Chromebooks not returned, late loaners and unpaid student shares.

### Settings

| Tab | What you change there |
|---|---|
| People | Who can open the app and with which role |
| School | School name, address, IT email, policy link, logo |
| Prices | Repair prices and who pays for the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and later repairs |
| Emails | The text of the first email, the reminder and the receipt, in English and Spanish |
| Other forms | How the Digital Promise and Verizon forms are handled |
| Technical | Set once at installation |

## IT Staff

You see Home, Students, Deployment day, and Chromebooks and repairs. You do not see parent phone numbers or emails unless an Admin has allowed it.

- **Find a student.** Go to Students and type a name or ID. The *Show* list narrows it to, for example, students who have not signed. Click a student to open their page.
- **Student page.** The top shows where the student is and one button for the usual next step. Everything else is under **More actions**: open or remake the PDF, copy the link, print a QR letter, new repair, report lost or stolen, Chromebook returned, Chromebook not returned. **History** at the bottom lists every step with date and name.
- **Signed forms.** A family that signs in the app completes all three forms at once, and the student is ready for a Chromebook straight away. On the student page, **More actions** opens each of the three PDFs. Once a Chromebook is assigned, it also opens the check-out record, a separate page the app saves by itself with the asset tag, serial number, who handed it over and when. The family's signed agreement is never changed after signing. In Drive they are in the student's own folder: `Grade N / LastName_FirstName_StudentID`.
- **Paper forms.** If a family hands in a Digital Promise or Verizon form on paper instead, set it to *Paper copy received and checked* on the student page.
- **Who got which Chromebook.** Chromebooks and repairs, **Check-outs** lists every Chromebook handed over: date and time, student, asset tag, serial number, and who checked it out. Filter by person, download it as a spreadsheet file, or (Admins) press **Save as a Google Sheet** to get a dated copy in the Drive folder.
- **Repairs.** Chromebooks and repairs, Repairs and lost reports, **New repair**. Later, click the repair, choose the part (the cost fills in), set the status and save. The app works out what the school and the student pay from the student's repair number.
- **Lost or stolen.** Press **Lost or stolen Chromebook**. The Chromebook is marked Lost and IT is emailed.
- **Loaners.** Chromebooks and repairs, Loaners. Choose the student, scan the loaner, press **Give out this loaner**. It is due the same day. Press **It is back** when it returns. Late loaners are shown in red.
- **Returns.** On the student page choose **Chromebook returned** when a student leaves. If it does not come back, choose **Chromebook not returned**. The student then appears on the records-on-hold report.
- **Forms students fill in themselves.** The links to the repair request and the lost report are at the bottom of Repairs and lost reports.

## Station Helper

Thank you for helping. Sign in with the school account you were given and open the staff link. You see one screen.

1. Type the student's name and tap the student.
2. Do what the screen says. It shows one of these:

| The screen says | What you do |
|---|---|
| **NOT READY**, and a **Sign now on this tablet** button | Tap it, tap **Open the signing form**, and hand the tablet to the parent. When they finish, come back and tap **Check again**. |
| **NOT READY**, a form is missing | Send the family to IT staff. Do not hand over a Chromebook. |
| **READY**, **Forms are complete. Continue** | Tap it. |
| A box for the asset tag | Explain the Chromebook Policy and the repair costs, tick the box, scan the tag on the Chromebook, and confirm the student's name. |
| **Ask the student to sign in** | If it works, tap **The student signed in and it works**. If not, tap **There is a problem**, write what is wrong, and send the student to the IT table. |
| **FINISHED** | Nothing more to do. |

3. Tap **Next student**.

If the screen says **STOP**, do not hand over the Chromebook. Call IT staff. Please do not share your sign-in.

At a three-station event every station uses this same screen. Station 1 handles signing and forms, Station 2 the asset tag, Station 3 the sign-in check.

## Parents (English)

You sign three forms in one sitting: the school's Chromebook Checkout form, the Digital Promise agreement and the Verizon General Release. It takes about ten minutes. Your student needs to be with you.

1. Open the email from the school and tap **Sign the agreement**, or scan the QR code on the letter with your phone camera. You do not need an account or a password.
2. Choose **English** or **Español** at the top.
3. **Step 1.** Read the Chromebook Checkout agreement and the repair costs. Tick the box at the bottom and tap **Continue**.
4. **Step 2.** Type your name, phone number and email.
5. **Step 3.** Read the Digital Promise agreement. Your student is the End User named in it.
6. **Step 4.** You and your student each type your initials, then tick both boxes on each of the nine lines.
7. **Step 5.** Read the Verizon General Release and choose **Agree and Consent** or **DO NOT Consent** (photos and video of your student). Either answer completes the form.
8. **Step 6.** Sign in the box with your finger. Tap **Clear** to try again. This one signature is placed on all three forms.
9. **Step 7.** Hand the phone to your student, who signs in the box. Tap **Sign and submit**.
10. Keep the page open until you see **Thank you**. Tap **Download** next to each form to save your copy. These are the same PDFs the school keeps. The three forms are also emailed to you.

You get one link for each child. A link works once. After signing it shows "Already signed", and for one hour it still lets you download your forms. If you lost the link or need help, write to itsupport@abqse.org.

## Padres (Español)

> Traducción de trabajo. Debe revisarla una persona hispanohablante de la escuela antes de enviarla.

Usted firma tres formularios de una sola vez: el formulario de préstamo de Chromebook de la escuela, el acuerdo de Digital Promise y la Autorización General de Verizon. Toma unos diez minutos. Su estudiante debe estar con usted. Los documentos de Digital Promise y Verizon solo están disponibles en inglés.

1. Abra el correo de la escuela y toque **Firmar el acuerdo**, o escanee el código QR de la carta con la cámara de su teléfono. No necesita cuenta ni contraseña.
2. Elija **English** o **Español** en la parte superior.
3. **Paso 1.** Lea el acuerdo de préstamo de Chromebook y los costos de reparación. Marque la casilla al final y toque **Continuar**.
4. **Paso 2.** Escriba su nombre, teléfono y correo electrónico.
5. **Paso 3.** Lea el acuerdo de Digital Promise.
6. **Paso 4.** Usted y su estudiante escriben sus iniciales y luego marcan las dos casillas en cada una de las nueve líneas.
7. **Paso 5.** Lea la Autorización General de Verizon y elija **Acepto y doy mi consentimiento** o **NO doy mi consentimiento** (fotos y videos de su estudiante). Cualquiera de las dos respuestas completa el formulario.
8. **Paso 6.** Firme en el recuadro con el dedo. Toque **Borrar** para intentarlo de nuevo. Esta firma se coloca en los tres formularios.
9. **Paso 7.** Pase el teléfono a su estudiante, que firma en el recuadro. Toque **Firmar y enviar**.
10. Mantenga la página abierta hasta ver **Gracias**. Toque **Descargar** junto a cada formulario para guardar su copia. Son los mismos PDF que guarda la escuela. También recibirá los tres formularios por correo.

Recibirá un enlace por cada hijo. Cada enlace funciona una sola vez. Después de firmar muestra "Ya firmado", y durante una hora todavía le permite descargar sus formularios. Si perdió el enlace o necesita ayuda, escriba a itsupport@abqse.org.
